# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

I want this project to be a welcoming, respectful, and constructive environment for everyone.

I expect all contributors, maintainers, users, and participants to interact professionally and in good faith.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Communicate constructively.
- Give and receive constructive feedback.
- Focus on ideas and technical decisions rather than individuals.
- Respect different levels of experience.
- Help maintain a welcoming environment.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Respect the project's scope and contribution guidelines.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks.
- Threats or intimidation.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Sexual harassment or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing private or sensitive information without permission.
- Deliberate impersonation.
- Spam or malicious behavior.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or project work.
- Any other behavior that would reasonably make the project unsafe or hostile for others.

## Technical Discussions

Technical disagreements are expected and can be healthy.

When disagreeing:

- Explain your reasoning.
- Provide evidence or examples when possible.
- Critique implementations and ideas rather than people.
- Be willing to change your position when presented with better information.
- Prefer maintainable and practical solutions over unnecessary complexity.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may take appropriate action in response to unacceptable behavior, including:

- Removing comments or contributions.
- Closing issues or discussions.
- Rejecting pull requests.
- Temporarily restricting participation.
- Permanently restricting participation in the project.

The response will depend on the severity and circumstances of the behavior.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the project maintainer.

Do not publicly expose sensitive information when making a report.

## Good-Faith Participation

Mistakes happen.

The goal of this Code of Conduct is not to discourage technical discussion or honest mistakes. It exists to establish a baseline of respect and make it easier for people to collaborate effectively.

Thank you for helping keep the project welcoming and constructive.
